Tip

Rua Direita runs from the main gate, Porta da Vila, up to the castle at the north end of the town.

Flights of stone steps lead up onto the walls. There is no railing on the town side, so mind the edge.

The long west wall looks over the Várzea plain. Come up in the late afternoon for the sunset.

Press T to let the day run, or set the time of day and the season in the settings (H).

Press F for the free camera. Press it again to drop back to your feet wherever you are.
