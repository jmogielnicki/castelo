# Audio credits

Every sound in this folder is a real recording from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), released under
**Creative Commons 0** (public domain dedication: no attribution required, but credited here anyway).
Nothing is synthesised. The files and their processing come from
[Tidewater](https://github.com/dgreenheck/tidewater)'s audio build. Each source is the sound's Freesound
high-quality preview.

Processing: excerpt, high-pass (some), trim, fades, an equal-power crossfade at the loop point, loudness
normalisation (loops about -23 LUFS integrated; one-shot slices peak-normalised to -1 dBFS), and Opus in Ogg
(64 kb/s). One-shot files are sprites; the slice table is in `src/audio/soundBank.js`.
